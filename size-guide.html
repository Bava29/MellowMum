A LITTLE EXTRA GUIDANCE

Questions About Your Fit?

Finding your size should feel simple. Here are a few helpful answers to guide you towards a more comfortable fit.

Still need a little help?

We're here to help you understand your measurements and find the right fit for your needs.

Contact Our Team →
Should I choose my usual clothing size?

Start by checking the size chart for the specific garment. Some maternity styles follow pre-pregnancy sizing, while others may have different fit guidance. Follow the product's own size information before choosing.

What if my measurements fall between two sizes?

Compare your bust, waist and hip measurements with the garment's size chart. Consider the fabric stretch and the fit you prefer. If you're still unsure, contact our team before placing your order.

How often should I take my measurements?

Your measurements may change throughout pregnancy. Recheck them whenever your current clothes start feeling different or before buying a new style, especially if you've noticed a change in fit.

Which measurements should I check first?

Check all the measurements listed on the product's size chart. Bust, waist and hips are common measurements, but the most important ones can vary by garment.

What should I do if my order doesn't fit comfortably?

Review the store's return and exchange policy before purchasing. If you need help, contact our team with the product name and your measurements so we can guide you through the available options.

Can I wear maternity clothes after pregnancy?

Yes! Many maternity styles are designed for comfort and flexibility, making them suitable for postpartum wear too. Check the individual product details to learn more about its fit and features.

What should I do if I am between two sizes?

Check our Size Guide and the product's fit details before choosing. Consider the garment's stretch, design, and your comfort. If you are still unsure, contact our team for help selecting a suitable size.
